HOW CAN WE HELP?

Meet Marian — ask in plain English and get an answer in seconds, 24/7, in any language.

What the assistant can do

🔎

Find & reserve, in plain English

Natural-language search over a read-only view of the catalog, with live availability across all four branches. Place holds and choose a pickup branch.

🔄

Interlibrary loan

Don't own it? The assistant drafts and submits an ILL request from a partner library — no desk visit required.

🧒

Age-appropriate suggestions

Tell it an age or grade and it recommends right-leveled titles, with content notes for caregivers.

🗓️

Events & story times

Find programs by age, branch, or day — story times, clubs, workshops — and set reminders.

🔬

Research help

Routes you to the right licensed databases (Ancestry, Gale, more) and augments with cited open-web sources.

🔐

Safe by design

Reads from a read-only replica with no customer PII. Holds & ILL go through Polaris's official APIs with full audit.
