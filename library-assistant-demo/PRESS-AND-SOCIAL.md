# Press & Social Copy — Library Magic Assistant

Ready-to-adapt copy to announce the pilot. **Honesty guardrail:** until it's live, say
"first to **pilot** / first to **build it together**" — true and still exciting. Don't
imply the service is already running. Swap in the final assistant name once chosen
("Marian" is a placeholder).

---

## Short press blurb (≈90 words)

> **Fort Smith Public Library to pilot first-of-its-kind AI library assistant**
>
> Fort Smith, AR — The Fort Smith Public Library is exploring a first-in-the-nation
> pilot: a friendly AI assistant that lets customers find and reserve books in plain
> language, check what's on the shelf across all four branches in real time, discover
> events and story times, and get research help — 24/7, in any language, with a
> librarian always one click away. Built on the library's existing catalog and
> databases, the assistant keeps customer privacy front and center. "We're not buying
> software off a shelf — we're building it together, for Fort Smith," said library
> leadership.

## Even shorter (≈40 words, for a newsletter)

> Coming soon to your front porch: a friendly library assistant you can just *talk* to.
> Find and reserve books, discover events, and get research help — 24/7, in any
> language. Fort Smith is proud to be the first to build it.
