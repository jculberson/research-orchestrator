# Demo Script & 30-Second Promo — Library Magic Assistant

Two things in here:
1. **Opening demo scenario** — the live, click-through walkthrough to open the meeting with Jennifer Goodson.
2. **30-second promo video script** — storyboard + voiceover, with the "first library to build it together" angle.

Every click below maps to a prompt the working prototype actually answers (`index.html` / the single-file build). Practice once so the beats land.

---

## 1. Opening demo scenario (~3–4 minutes)

**Goal of the open:** in under a minute, show a real patron task — *find a book, see it's live on the shelf, reserve it* — then fan out into the breadth. Lead with the "wow," then prove the depth.

**Setup:** Open the demo full-screen on the branded homepage. Have it already loaded so the Fort Smith look (navy logo, "Your Community Front Porch," teal tiles) is the first thing she sees. Don't open the chat yet.

### The cold open (say this)

> "This is your website — same look, same 'Your Community Front Porch.' The only thing that's new is right here: **'How can we help?'** Today that's a search box. Imagine it could actually *answer.* Watch."

Click **✨ Start a conversation** (or the **Ask Marian** button).

### Beat 1 — Find it, see it's really on the shelf  *(the wow)*

- **Click the chip:** `🔎 Find me a book about dragons`
- **What she sees:** book cards (*Fourth Wing*, *Iron Flame*) with **live availability across all four branches** — copies in vs. out.
- **Say:** "Plain English in. And this isn't a guess — it's reading live availability at Main, Dallas, Miller, and Windsor Drive."

### Beat 2 — Reserve it  *(close the loop)*

- **Type:** `Reserve The Women for pickup`
- **Click** "Place hold & pick a branch" → choose **Main Library**.
- **What she sees:** a hold confirmation with pickup branch and "ready in 1–2 days."
- **Say:** "She just placed a hold — in conversation, no catalog login, no desk visit. In the real build that hold goes through Polaris's official system, fully logged."

### Beat 3 — Take care of a parent  *(emotional beat)*

- **Click the tile / type:** `Book ideas for my 7-year-old`
- **What she sees:** right-leveled picks with a caregiver content note.
- **Say:** "A parent at 9 p.m. gets age-appropriate suggestions — with content notes — without waiting for the desk to open."

### Beat 4 — Research help + the breadth  *(depth)*

- **Click the tile:** `🌳 GENEALOGY` (sends "Help me research my family tree")
- **What she sees:** the right licensed databases (Ancestry, Gale…) **plus cited open-web sources**.
- **Say:** "It routes people to the databases you already pay for — and adds *cited* sources, never made-up answers."
- **Optional fan-out (one click each if time):** `🕐 When's the next story time?` · `🗓️ What's happening this week?` · `🔄` an interlibrary-loan request for a title you don't own.

### The close (say this)

> "Everything I clicked is sample data in a prototype — but every piece is built on systems you already have: your catalog in Polaris, your branches, your databases. We're not replacing your website or your staff. We're adding a friendly front door — 24/7, in any language — and a human is always one click away. And here's the part I'm most excited about: **no library in the country has this yet. Fort Smith could be the first — and help shape it.**"

**Then transition to the proposal / cost appendix.**

### Presenter tips
- If a click misfires, just retype the prompt in the box — it's forgiving.
- Keep the **"read-only, no patron data, cited, human one click away"** line handy — it answers the trust question before it's asked.
- Resist showing *everything.* Four beats land harder than ten.

## 2. 30-second promo video script

**Format:** ~30s, screen-recording of the prototype as b-roll + on-screen captions + warm VO + light, hopeful music. Brand palette: navy `#3b4a78`, mauve `#854c5e`, teal `#4e8a9c`, coral `#d9543b`.

> **Note:** This is a *concept promo* for an unannounced prototype. Produce/publish only once the partnership is greenlit; keep a "concept" disclaimer on any internal cut.

**Voiceover wordcount:** ~78 words (~30s at a natural pace).

| Time | Visual (b-roll) | On-screen caption | Voiceover |
|------|------------------|-------------------|-----------|
| 0:00–0:04 | Fort Smith homepage; cursor lands on **"How can we help?"** | *Your library. Now with answers.* | "Your library has always been your community's front porch." |
| 0:04–0:09 | Type "find me a book about dragons" → cards with live availability appear | *Ask in plain English.* | "Now just ask — in plain English." |
| 0:09–0:14 | "Reserve The Women" → pick a branch → ✅ hold confirmed | *Reserve it — live, across every branch.* | "Find it, see it's on the shelf, and reserve it — in seconds." |
| 0:14–0:19 | "Book ideas for my 7-year-old" → kid-level picks | *Just-right reads for every age.* | "Suggestions for every reader. Research help. Events and story times." |
| 0:19–0:23 | Genealogy tile → databases + cited sources | *Real sources. Always cited.* | "Grounded in your collections — never made up." |
| 0:23–0:27 | Quick montage: hours, events, ILL, languages | *24/7 · any language · a human one click away* | "Open all night. In any language. A librarian always one click away." |
| 0:27–0:30 | Fort Smith logo + tagline lockup; assistant sparkle | **Fort Smith Public Library** — *first in the nation to build it.* | "Fort Smith Public Library — the first to bring it home." |

### Alt closing lines (pick the vibe)
- "**Built here first. Built together.**"
- "**A national first — proudly from Fort Smith.**"
- "**The future of the library — and we're building it together.**"

### Production notes
- **Capture b-roll** by screen-recording the real prototype (the single-file build runs offline) so the footage matches the demo exactly.
- **Music:** warm, optimistic, light percussion; resolve on the logo beat.
- **Captions are load-bearing** — most social views are muted; make sure the feature words read on their own.
- **Two cuts:** a 30s social cut (this) and a 15s teaser (beats 1, 2, and the close).

### Hype / partnership messaging bank (for posts, slides, press)
- "Fort Smith Public Library — **the first library in the nation** to pilot an AI library assistant, built side-by-side with our community in mind."
- "We're not buying software off a shelf. **We're building it together** — shaped by Fort Smith, for Fort Smith."
- "A pioneering partnership: world-class AI meets the **'Community Front Porch.'**"
- "Proudly built in Arkansas. **Open to everyone, day or night.**"
- "Your card already unlocks more than you know. Now it's as easy as asking."

> Keep public claims honest: it's a **pilot/concept** until launched. "First to pilot / first to build it together" is the safe, true framing — avoid implying it's already live.
